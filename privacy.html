Privacy notice

Families deserve clarity and care.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

About this notice

This notice explains the current information practices for sparoahelle.com. The website is an informational launch page for Sparoah Elle and does not currently provide child accounts, online purchases, or direct child-facing digital experiences.

Information we receive

This website does not currently use an embedded contact form, advertising tracker, or analytics tool. Our website host may process limited technical information—such as an IP address, browser type, device information, and request time—in ordinary server and security logs. When an adult chooses to contact us by email, we receive the information included in that message, such as the adult’s name, email address, and questions.

Children’s information

This website is directed to parents and caregivers, not children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through this website. Adults should not email sensitive or unnecessary information about a child, including photos, videos, voice recordings, school information, health information, or precise location.

How email information is used

Information sent by email may be used to respond to an inquiry, share requested information about a future Founding Family pilot, maintain a record of the conversation, and protect the security and integrity of the business.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate business communications, comply with law, prevent harm, or protect legal rights.

Data choices

An adult may ask us to stop future communications or delete an inquiry from our active records, subject to legal, security, and recordkeeping obligations.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated before the website adds purchasing, waitlist forms, analytics, personalized fulfillment, or other information practices not described here.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to privacy@sparoahelle.com.
