What we mean by “Queen”

Not status. Stewardship. The Queen within is the dignity to know her worth, the courage to act on her ideas, the wisdom to choose well, the creativity to build, and the compassion to use her gifts in service to others.

The story behind Sparoah Elle

It began with one unexpected birthday answer.

As Gregory’s daughter approached her eighth birthday, he offered her two summer experiences: gymnastics and a children’s AI class. When asked what she wanted, she chose both—and explained why:

“Because I want to start my own company.”

That answer revealed something bigger. Girls often carry ideas long before adults create intentional space for them to explore those ideas. Sparoah Elle was created to help families make that space—together.

Parents often see the spark—the questions, sketches, plans, and possibilities—but do not always have a clear way to help an idea move forward without taking it over. A Quest gives families an intentional path to listen, encourage, and help one idea take its next meaningful step.

What your family receives

Planned components of The Power Within

These components are planned for the Founding Family Pilot and may be refined through family feedback.

  • A personalized opening story

    A meaningful starting point that helps the experience feel made for her.

  • Parent conversation prompts

    Simple guidance that makes room for listening, encouragement, and honest connection.

  • An idea-to-action challenge

    A creative task that moves her from “I wonder” to “I made this.”

  • Reflection and Quest Passport

    A place to name what she discovered, practiced, and wants to carry forward.

  • A service step

    An invitation to use what she learned to encourage, help, or create for someone else.

  • A completion keepsake

    A lasting reminder that her ideas, voice, effort, and contribution matter.

Our promise to families

Childhood should be protected while possibility is expanded.

Technology may support the work behind the scenes, but the relationship remains human. Parents guide the experience. Children are not handed over to an open-ended AI companion.

✓

Parent-led by design

The adult remains the guide, listener, and trusted presence throughout the Quest.

✓

Human-reviewed personalization

Child-facing personalized materials are reviewed by a person before they reach a family.

✓

Minimal information

We ask adults only for what is needed and do not invite children to create accounts on this site.

✓

No direct marketing to children

We market to adults, not to children. Parents and caregivers decide whether and how a child participates.
