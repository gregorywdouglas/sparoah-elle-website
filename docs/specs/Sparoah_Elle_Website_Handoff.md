## Founding story approved for the site

Before her birthday, Sparoah was offered two summer experiences: gymnastics and a children’s AI class. When asked what she wanted, she chose both and explained:

> “Because I want to start my own company.”

The company insight is that girls often carry ideas long before adults create intentional space for them to explore those ideas. Sparoah Elle is intended to help families make that space together.

Use the story with restraint. It should establish authenticity without making Sparoah responsible for publicly performing, operating, or carrying the commercial success of the business.

## Family and child-safety commitments

These are foundational, not optional marketing claims:

- Parent-led by design
- Human review of child-facing personalization
- Minimal information collection
- No direct marketing to children
- No child account at launch
- No child-facing open-ended chatbot
- No unnecessary collection of child photos, video, voice, or detailed profiles
- No unsupported claims about educational, psychological, mental-health, or developmental outcomes
- Technology may assist behind the scenes, but the relationship remains human

The site currently contains no analytics, tracking pixels, embedded intake form, or child-facing functionality.

## Important truthfulness constraints

The website must not:

- Suggest the company has customers, revenue, completed pilots, endorsements, or measurable outcomes that do not yet exist
- Suggest Good Soil, Wells Fargo, T.D. Jakes, or another party endorses or sponsors Sparoah Elle
- Present Sparoah as the legal operator, adult founder, CEO, or competition representative
- Add “LLC” until registration is confirmed
- Guarantee confidence, learning, development, healing, or family outcomes
- Describe AI as replacing parents, advisors, or human review
- Build the appearance of scale before real customer evidence exists

## Privacy and compliance notes

The included privacy page is a launch-stage notice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before:

- Collecting payments
- Using analytics or advertising pixels
- Embedding a waitlist or pilot intake form
- Collecting personalization information
- Collecting any child-related data

Until a secure intake process is approved, adults are instructed not to email personal information about a child.

## Deployment baseline

The website is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript package. It can be deployed to any static host.

Required deployment behavior:

- Web root must contain `index.html`
- Connect both `sparoahelle.com` and `www.sparoahelle.com`
- Use `https://sparoahelle.com/` as the canonical address
- Redirect the `www` version to the root domain
- Enable HTTPS
- Verify desktop and mobile behavior after launch
- Test every navigation link, email CTA, privacy link, favicon, and social-sharing image

The exact deployment instructions depend on where the domain was purchased and which host is selected.
